About Liuyi

Hi! I’m an undergraduate HCI researcher at Whitman College. My research focuses on designing interactive technologies for reflection, sensemaking, and slow, embodied experiences. I use research-through-design as my primary methodology. This website showcases my work and exploration in interactions, art, and comparative psychology.
