Ziheng “Liuyi” Lyu

Hi! I do design research and (sometimes) make art. I'm an undergraduate at Whitman College, double-majoring in psychology and computer science. My research interests are in human-computer interaction (HCI), particularly research-through-design and reflective design.

I'm curious about aspects of human experience that feel deeply personal yet strangely universal. These include remembering and forgetting, temporality, emotions, self-reflection, sensory experiences, relationships, and many other facets of what makes us human.

This website is a collection of my work and explorations.
