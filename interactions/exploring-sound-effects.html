Exploring Sound Effects (summer 25)

I designed Exploring Sound Effects, a 60-minute workshop activity for CS-100-A ST Your Life in Sound: Listening with Your Ears and with Technology, Fall 2025, taught by Prof. Jordan Wirfs-Brock at Whitman College. It asks how audio effects can change the character, feeling, and language people use to describe a sound.

Participants bring a familiar vocal clip and non-vocal clip, each at least twenty seconds long, then listen closely and develop descriptors for each one. The descriptors can name an atmosphere, image, sensation, emotion, style, or other quality that emerges through listening.

Using an online sound-effects tool, participants manipulate their recordings until their initial descriptors no longer fit. They then create a new set of descriptors and reflect on what changed, including the moods, memories, and imagined scenes prompted by the altered sound. The activity uses transformation as a way to make listening more deliberate and descriptive.
