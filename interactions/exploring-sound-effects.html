Exploring Sound Effects

Summer 2025

I designed this 60-minute listening activity in summer 2025 for Jordan Wirfs-Brock’s fall 2025 course Your Life in Sound at Whitman College. It explores how audio effects change the way a sound feels and the words people use to describe it.

Students bring a familiar vocal clip and non-vocal clip, each at least twenty seconds long, then listen closely and describe what each evokes. These descriptions might refer to an atmosphere or an imagined scene.

Using an online sound-effects tool, students manipulate their recordings until their initial descriptions no longer fit. They describe the altered sounds and reflect on how their associations have changed. The goal of this activity is to make listening more deliberate, descriptive, and playful by making familiar sounds strange.
