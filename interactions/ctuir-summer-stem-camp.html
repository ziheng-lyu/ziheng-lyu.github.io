CTUIR Summer STEM Camp

Summer 2025

I co-facilitated a week of sound and listening activities designed by my colleagues for the Confederated Tribes of the Umatilla Indian Reservation (CTUIR) summer STEM camp. We worked with elementary students at Washington Elementary School in Pendleton, Oregon.

Students identified objects by sound and collected recordings on listening walks. They experimented with audio effects and used sound to represent data. We finished the week by building elastic-band guitars and exploring how vibration produces sound.
