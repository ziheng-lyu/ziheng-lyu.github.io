Audible Feelings (Summer 25-Present)

What might a mood-journal entry sound like? Audible Feelings is a mood-tracking and micro-journaling app that turns short, metaphorical reflections into AI-generated sound. The project began with an interest in moving beyond the graphs and categories common in mood trackers toward a more sensory, open-ended way of attending to everyday feelings.

A person records a simple mood log, then answers a sensory prompt such as what object, environment, texture, or creature might resemble their feeling. These "funky" prompts encourage people to think about their feelings metaphorically. A text-to-sound model turns the response into a short audio clip, shaped by a sonic label such as “ambient soundscape,” “piano solo,” or no label at all.

Through first-person use and a diary study with seven participants, we explored the role of generative sound in mood journaling practice. We approached the app as a technology probe for asking what sound can make possible in reflective journaling.

Related Publication: Ziheng Lyu and Jordan Wirfs-Brock. 2026. Audible Feelings: Exploring the Role of Generative Sound in Reflective Mood Journaling. In Adjunct Proceedings of the 14th Nordic Conference on Human-Computer Interaction (NordiCHI ’26 Adjunct), October 03–07, 2026, Vaasa, Finland.
