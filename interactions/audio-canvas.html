Audio Canvas

Spring 2026 · Early exploration

What might a sketch sound like? Audio Canvas is an early idea for using generative sound as part of sketching. I’m curious about how things with no obvious sound, such as a lotus or a feeling of solitude, might be interpreted through audio.

I briefly tried having a vision-language model describe sketches, then passing those descriptions to a text-to-sound model. I also wrote prompts from my own interpretations. This remains a rough exploration, with an open question about how listening might influence what I draw next.
